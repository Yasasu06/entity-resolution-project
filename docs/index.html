What went wrong, found before checking

The investigation

The system’s central weakness was found, measured and published while the labels were still sealed — using two methods that needed no ground truth.

Method one — an independent judge

A language model was shown 704 of the auto-accepted pairs and asked to decide them cold, without seeing any score. It disagreed with 31%. Disagreement fell steadily with score, from 72.6% in the lowest band to 11.2% in the highest — the signature of real signal rather than a capricious judge.

Method two — planted near-misses

For every accepted pair, its partner was cloned with exactly one attribute changed so the clone was definitely a different product. Ground truth by construction, no judgement involved. The clone tied or beat the true partner 49% of the time, and the median gap was zero bits.

The mechanism

Every comparison measures token overlap. A mutated token joins the set rather than displacing anything, so the shared evidence is unchanged and the score does not move.

Where both methods were blind

The probes were built by mutating partners of already-accepted pairs, so every probe had a true partner by construction. Both methods therefore missed the dominant failure entirely: records that have no partner at all. Only 852 of 2,554 records have one, and the system accepted 994.

The clearest single error WALMART   pny geforce gt 520 1024mb pcie graphics card CHOSEN    pny gt 430 1024mb ddr3 …   12.7 bits CORRECT   pny nvidia geforce gt520 1gb …    8.0 bits The wrong answer scored higher than the right one.

The error decomposition inverted the project’s priorities. Of 456 wrong accepts, 413 were records with no partner and only 43 were cases where a partner existed and the wrong one was chosen. Three separate investigations had gone after the 9%.

Two fixes followed. A quantity veto — refusing pairs whose stated capacities or dimensions contradict — reaches 1.5% of accepts, and that honest figure is reported rather than the 9.6% the probes implied. A mutual-best-match requirement removed 62 accepts that were wrong 93.5% of the time. Raising the threshold, even tuned with full label access, gained 0.6 F1: a precision-for-recall trade, not an improvement.

How it was kept honest

The seal

No labelled data was read until the whole system was finished. That was enforced in code, not by intention: the loader refuses to open a labelled split without an explicit override, and the refusal has its own test.

Every threshold, the review interface’s display rules, the AI reviewer’s exact prompt and model, and the full list of measurements were fixed in a pre-registration document and committed before the answer key was opened. Corrections are made by adding new entries, never by revising old ones.
